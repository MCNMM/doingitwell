# Daily organic lead system for Southern Nevada Health Pass

## Objective
Find 10 qualified online client leads per day who identify as Molina Healthcare of Nevada, SilverSummit Healthplan, or Nevada Medicaid Fee-for-Service members.

## Daily scorecard
| Channel | Daily action | Lead target |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Facebook groups | 10-15 helpful comments or approved posts in Las Vegas, Henderson, North Las Vegas, Clark County, pregnancy, disability, housing, food assistance, and Medicaid groups | 3-5 |
| Short-form video | Publish 1-2 videos to TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, and YouTube Shorts | 2-4 |
| Google Business Profile | Keep services, photos, Q&A, reviews, and weekly posts active | 2-3 |
| Reddit/Nextdoor | Answer 5 benefit-related questions without asking for public private health information | 1-2 |
| Partner outreach | Message 5 community pages, churches, food pantries, WIC-adjacent pages, recovery groups, school resource pages, or nonprofits | 1-3 |

## Core message
Have Molina, SilverSummit, or Nevada Medicaid Fee-for-Service? You may have free benefits you are not using, such as rides, wellness rewards, OTC items, pregnancy resources, gym resources, and more depending on your plan. Southern Nevada Health Pass helps members check benefits for free. Call or text 702-867-1223.

## Daily workflow
1. Check inboxes and missed calls before posting new content.
2. Post one plan-specific benefit prompt in one approved local group.
3. Comment helpfully on 10 local posts where people ask about Medicaid, rides, doctors, pregnancy resources, food assistance, disability help, or insurance cards.
4. Publish one short video using a local keyword caption.
5. Send five partner outreach messages.
6. Log every lead source, plan type, phone number, opt-in status, and next action in the tracker.
7. Follow up with every same-day lead before close of business.

## Qualification questions
- Which card do you have: Molina, SilverSummit, Nevada Medicaid Fee-for-Service, or not sure?
- Are you in Southern Nevada or Clark County?
- What benefit are you trying to check: rides, wellness visit, rewards, pregnancy resources, OTC, provider, or something else?
- Is it okay if Southern Nevada Health Pass calls or texts you about your benefits question?

## Compliance guardrails
- Use "may qualify" and "depending on your plan" instead of guaranteeing benefits.
- Do not claim to represent Molina, SilverSummit, or Nevada Medicaid unless written authorization exists.
- Do not ask members to post Medicaid IDs, birth dates, diagnoses, or card photos publicly.
- Move sensitive conversations to a private, approved intake channel.
- Confirm benefits with the plan or Nevada Medicaid before telling a member a benefit is available.
