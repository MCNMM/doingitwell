# Autonomous lead generation operating playbook

## What is automated now
- Daily task queue generation for organic lead sources.
- Lead scoring based on consent, phone number, target plan, need, and source quality.
- Same-day follow-up task creation after every saved lead.
- Plan-specific message generation for Facebook groups, helpful replies, partners, Google Business Profile, and short-form video.
- CSV export for lead backup, reporting, and CRM import.

## What becomes fully automated in Lovable
- Persistent database storage through Lovable Cloud or Supabase.
- Authenticated staff dashboard.
- Scheduled daily queue creation at 8:00 AM Pacific.
- Automated next-day reminders for unreached leads.
- Weekly source-performance reports.
- Optional approved integrations for SMS, email, Zapier/Make, and partner workflows.

## Daily autonomous loop
1. Open `autonomous-lead-system.html`.
2. The system creates the current day's task queue automatically.
3. Generate and post compliant channel messages.
4. Enter every inbound comment, DM, call, or checklist request as a lead.
5. Work follow-up tasks from highest score to lowest score.
6. Export CSV at the end of the day.
7. Compare channel performance against the 10-lead target.

## Human approval required
The system should not auto-post to Facebook groups, Reddit, Nextdoor, or Google Business Profile unless SNHP has approved platform-specific automation and the account owner has confirmed compliance with each platform's terms. Until then, it autonomously prepares the queue and copy while a human posts or approves each item.

## Privacy rules
- Do not collect Medicaid IDs in the lead tracker.
- Do not collect diagnoses.
- Do not collect card photos in public or non-approved systems.
- Do not collect full dates of birth.
- Record consent before calling or texting.
- Confirm benefits directly with the plan or Nevada Medicaid.
