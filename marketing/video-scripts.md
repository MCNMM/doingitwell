# Short-form video scripts

## Script 1: Plan benefit check
If you have Molina, SilverSummit, or Nevada Medicaid Fee-for-Service in Southern Nevada, you might be missing free benefits. Southern Nevada Health Pass helps you check rewards, rides, wellness visit options, and plan extras. Text your insurance plan name to 702-867-1223 and we will help you for free.

## Script 2: Molina
Molina Medicaid members in Las Vegas: ask about transportation, wellness visit rewards, pregnancy resources, and other plan extras. Benefits depend on your eligibility and current plan rules, but Southern Nevada Health Pass can help you prepare the right questions. Text MOLINA to 702-867-1223.

## Script 3: SilverSummit
SilverSummit members: before your next appointment, check whether your plan has transportation support, wellness resources, rewards, or pregnancy benefits. Southern Nevada Health Pass helps local members review benefit questions for free. Text SILVERSUMMIT to 702-867-1223.

## Script 4: Fee-for-Service
Do you know whether your Nevada Medicaid is Fee-for-Service or managed care? If you are not sure, Southern Nevada Health Pass can help you review your card and notices. Text FFS to 702-867-1223.

## Script 5: Privacy reminder
Quick reminder: never post your Medicaid card, full date of birth, or private health information in public comments. If you need benefit help, Southern Nevada Health Pass can guide you through a private intake. Text CHECKLIST to 702-867-1223.

## Script 6: Pregnant members
Pregnant or recently had a baby and have Molina, SilverSummit, or Nevada Medicaid? Ask about pregnancy rewards, rides, prenatal care, postpartum support, and baby resources. Text BABY to 702-867-1223.

## Script 7: Rides
Need a ride to a covered medical appointment? Molina, SilverSummit, and Nevada Medicaid programs can have different transportation rules. Southern Nevada Health Pass helps you figure out what to ask and who to call. Text RIDES to 702-867-1223.

## Script 8: Provider questions
Before scheduling, confirm the provider accepts your exact plan and ask whether a referral or prior authorization is needed. Need help understanding your card? Text PLAN to 702-867-1223.

## Script 9: Wellness visit
Your annual wellness or preventive visit may connect to plan resources or rewards depending on your coverage. Ask before you go. Southern Nevada Health Pass can help you prepare. Text WELLNESS to 702-867-1223.

## Script 10: Checklist
Want the 2026 Southern Nevada Medicaid Benefits Checklist? Text CHECKLIST to 702-867-1223. It covers Molina, SilverSummit, Fee-for-Service, rides, wellness resources, pregnancy support, OTC questions, and privacy reminders.
