2026 Southern Nevada Medicaid Benefits Checklist

Use this checklist before calling your plan, scheduling care, or contacting Southern Nevada Health Pass. Text CHECKLIST to 702-867-1223 for help.

Plan identification

  • Molina Healthcare of Nevada card
  • SilverSummit Healthplan card
  • Nevada Medicaid Fee-for-Service card
  • Recent Medicaid or managed care notices
  • Unsure which plan? Ask for a card review.

Benefits to ask about

  • Transportation or rides for covered appointments
  • Wellness or preventive visit rewards
  • Over-the-counter or health-related extras
  • Pregnancy, postpartum, and baby resources
  • Gym, YMCA, fitness, or healthy lifestyle resources
  • Food, grocery, phone, or community resource referrals

Before an appointment

  • Confirm the provider accepts your exact plan.
  • Ask whether a referral or prior authorization is needed.
  • Ask how to schedule transportation, if available.
  • Ask whether the visit qualifies for plan rewards.
  • Write down the representative name and reference number.

Private information safety

  • Do not post your Medicaid ID publicly.
  • Do not post your full date of birth publicly.
  • Send card photos only through private, approved channels.
  • Ask how your information will be stored and protected.

Call-to-action

Call or text 702-867-1223. Message prompts: MOLINA, SILVERSUMMIT, FFS, or CHECKLIST.

This checklist is educational and does not guarantee benefits. Confirm current coverage, eligibility, providers, and rewards directly with the plan or Nevada Medicaid.
